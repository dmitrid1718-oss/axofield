AXOFIELD

A quiet instrument for attention.

Most of the time, we move through life without fully arriving in it. AxoField creates a small interruption in automatic living — a responsive field quiet enough to remain at the edge of awareness, but alive enough to call you back.

Enter with intention. The field will not do the work for you. Do not look for a miracle or try to force an experience. Notice. Return. Stay with what is actually happening.

Keep it beside you. Glance at it or don’t. Let it remain in the periphery.

When you remember the field, remember yourself.

Camera and microphone are optional, off by default, and processed locally on your device. No video or audio is recorded, stored, or uploaded. No account is required.

This is an experimental visual instrument, not a medical, diagnostic, therapeutic, or safety device.
