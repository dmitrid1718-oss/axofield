AXOFIELD

A responsive visual field for movement and sound.

AxoField can use your camera and microphone to respond to movement, music, typing, voices, and other activity in the room.

Camera and microphone processing happens locally on your device.
No video or audio is recorded, stored, or uploaded by AxoField.

No account is required.

This is an experimental visual instrument, not a medical, diagnostic, therapeutic, or safety device.
